1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SYSTEM PURPOSE
================================================================================
ParishServe is a comprehensive, enterprise-grade ecclesiastical administration
and parish management platform designed specifically for Catholic parishes in the
Philippines under Canon Law (Code of Canon Law / Codex Iuris Canonici).

The system seamlessly unifies seven core operational pillars:
1. Canonical Sacramental Records Digitization (Canon 535) & Dynamic PDF/QR Certificate Generation
2. Parish Service Appointments, Mass Intention Scheduling & LitCal Calendar Integration
3. POS Cashiering, Official Receipts, & Particulars Catalog Management
4. Diocesan Asset Inventory, Reference Data Management, Security Watermarking & Asset Label Printing (CustodiaIMS)
5. Smart Archive Environmental Telemetry (ESP32 IoT Temperature & Relative Humidity Conservation)
6. Daily Catholic Mass Readings & Roman Catholic Lectionary Integration
7. Multi-Tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Governance & Security Auditing

================================================================================
2. ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC) GOVERNANCE
================================================================================
ParishServe enforces strict role-based access control across all screens,
database queries (RLS), and API endpoints:

1. Super Administrator ('superadmin' / S)
   - Unrestricted root access across all modules, database tables, and settings.
   - User account provisioning, role reassignment, account archiving, and hard deletion.
   - Global parish branding, certificate templates, and receipt configurations.

2. Administrator ('admin' / A)
   - Comprehensive administrative access to user accounts, asset inventory, and IoT archive telemetry.
   - Restricted from executing superadmin-only database overrides.

3. Parish Priest ('parishpriest' / P)
   - Supreme ecclesiastical authority and canonical overseer.
   - Full access to all Sacramental Registers (Canon 535), Tuesday appointment approvals, mass intentions, pastoral audit logs, and official certificate signatory approvals.

4. Parish Secretary ('secretary' / Sc)
   - Primary operational user for day-to-day parish administration.
   - Manages POS cashiering, official receipt issuance, appointment bookings, sacramental record entry, asset audits, asset label printing, and certificate generation.

5. Records Encoder ('encoder' / E)
   - Data entry specialist focused on field digitization of physical ledger books (Liber Baptismorum, etc.) and physical asset inventory tagging.

6. Parish Finance Council Auditor ('pfc' / PFC)
   - Financial auditor access for financial receipt reviews, particulars catalog oversight, cashiering reports, and diocesan asset valuation.

7. Parishioner / Client ('user' / U)
   - Self-service portal access for parishioners to book parish appointments, request mass intentions, view personal receipt transaction histories, read daily Catholic mass scriptures, and verify official certificate authenticity via QR code tokens.

4. CANONICAL SACRAMENTAL RECORDS & CERTIFICATE GENERATION MODULE
================================================================================
The Sacramental Records Module digitizes physical ledger books in accordance with
Canon 535 of the Code of Canon Law:
- Liber Baptismorum (Baptisms)
- Liber Confirmatorum (Confirmations)
- Liber Primae Communionis (First Holy Communions)
- Liber Matrimoniorum (Matrimonies)
- Liber Defunctorum (Deaths & Burials)
- Liber Conversorum (Conversions & Receptions into Full Communion)

Key Features & Rules:
1. Physical Coordinates Bound Validation:
   - Book Number: Integer 1 - 200
   - Page Number: Integer 1 - 100
   - Line Number: Integer 1 - 10
   - Prevents duplicate book/page/line registration entries.

2. Canon 877 §2 Compliance:
   - Handles unwed / unacknowledged paternity safely ("Not Indicated" checkbox fills canonical placeholders and displays "—" on certificates).

3. Certificate Generation Engine:
   - Supports Simple Mode (Structured word-processor flow with safe margins) and Canva-Style Visual Designer Mode (drag-and-drop element positioning, resizable text boxes, font selector, and color swatches).
   - Generates PDF documents using `pdf` and `printing` packages with TrueType Unicode fonts (via `pdf_google_fonts` - Arimo, Cousine, Cinzel, EB Garamond, Caveat, Tinos) to eliminate missing glyph errors for Special Characters (Ñ, ñ, diacritics) and currency symbols.

4. Dynamic Verification QR Tokenization:
   - Every issued certificate embeds a non-sequential, cryptographically random UUID v4 Verification Token (`verification_id`).
   - The embedded QR code points to the public verification web portal (`https://parishserve.web.app/verify?v=<TOKEN>`).
   - Anyone scanning the QR code can verify the recipient name, sacrament type, issue date, book reference, and status ('Valid' vs 'Revoked').

5. PARISH APPOINTMENTS & SCHEDULING MODULE
================================================================================
The Appointments Module manages scheduling for weddings, baptisms, funeral masses,
sick calls/anointing, house blessings, and thanksgiving mass intentions.

Service Presets & Durations:
- Nuptial Mass (Wedding): 90 minutes (1 hr 30 mins)
- Community Baptism: 60 minutes (1 hour)
- Private Baptism: 45 minutes
- Funeral Mass & Blessing: 60 minutes (1 hour)
- Anointing of the Sick & Viaticum: 45 minutes
- House / Business Blessing: 45 minutes
- Thanksgiving Mass Intention: 60 minutes
- Canonical Interview / Pre-Cana: 45 minutes
- Confession & Spiritual Direction: 30 minutes

Paramount Canonical Rules & Automated Validation:
1. Monday Prohibition (Clergy Rest Day):
   - Mondays are strictly designated as Canonical Clergy Rest Days and Parish Office closure. No routine appointments or office services can be booked on Mondays.

2. Tuesday Parish Priest Approval Rule:
   - Appointments booked on Tuesdays are automatically assigned a 'pending' status and require explicit approval from the Parish Priest before confirmation.

3. Liturgical Law Prohibition (Solemnity Blocking):
   - Automated blocking prevents scheduling appointments on Catholic Solemnities, Paschal Triduum (Holy Thursday, Good Friday, Holy Saturday), Easter Sunday, Ash Wednesday, All Saints Day (Nov 1), All Souls Day (Nov 2), Christmas Day (Dec 25), Mary Mother of God (Jan 1), Feast of Santo Niño, Immaculate Conception (Dec 8), and Parish Titular Solemnities (St. John Paul II, Oct 22).
   - Integrates with the LitCal Philippine National Liturgical Calendar API (`https://litcal.johnromanodorazio.com/api/dev/calendar/nation/PH`) with built-in Philippine National defaults.

4. Operating Hours:
   - Parish services must be scheduled between 06:00 AM and 07:00 PM.

5. Collision Detection:
   - Prevents overlapping bookings for the same venue or same presider/priest.

6. Automated Email Confirmation (Supabase Edge Function & Brevo):
   - Invokes `send-booking-confirmation` Deno Edge Function via Brevo SMTP API.
   - Automatically attaches canonical document submission checklists based on the service type.

6. CASHIERING, RECEIPTS & FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT MODULE
================================================================================
The Receipts Module handles financial cashiering, mass intention offerings,
and official receipt issuance.

Key Features:
- Particulars Catalog (`ParticularsService`): Configurable list of services and standard fee amounts (Sacraments, Mass Intentions, Certificates, Devotionals, Others).
- POS Cashiering Interface (`PosCashierPage`): Point-of-Sale shopping cart UI supporting Cash (with cash tendered & change calculation), GCash / E-Wallet (with reference number validation), and Gratis (canonically exempt).
- Sequential Receipt Numbering (`SecretaryService`): Sequential format `REC-YYYY-XXXXX`.
- Customizable Receipt Templates (`ReceiptTemplateService` & `ReceiptCanvasDesignerPage`): Supports Ecclesiastical Voucher Slip (8.5 × 4.125 in), A4, Letter, Legal (8.5 × 13 in) Folio, and 80mm Thermal Receipt Rolls.
- Multi-Format Printing Engine (`ReceiptPdfGenerator`): Generates PDF slips with amount-in-words conversion, itemized line items, TrueType fonts, and QR verification tokens (`https://parishserve.web.app/verify-receipt?rec=<REC_NO>`).

7. DIOCESAN ASSET INVENTORY MODULE (CustodiaIMS)
================================================================================
The Asset Inventory Module provides diocesan property tracking, reference data management, asset valuation, security watermarking, QR label printing, and mobile field auditing.

Key Features:
- Diocesan Control Number System: Automatically generated continuous control numbers `Location-Classification Year-Sequence` (e.g. `C-SI-2026-001`).
- Book of Inventory Section Classification:
  * Section 1: Acquisition price per unit ≥ ₱10,000.00
  * Section 2: Acquisition price per unit < ₱10,000.00
- Reference Management (`AssetReferenceService`): Dynamic locations and classifications (Sacred Image, Sacred Vessel, Musical Instrument, Liturgical Book, Furniture, Office Equipment, Electrical, Hand Tools, Others).
- Automated Security Watermarking (`AssetImageWatermarkUtil`): Embeds native canvas security watermark onto uploaded asset photos containing parish name, timestamp, and Diocesan Control #.
- Asset Label PDF Generation (`AssetLabelPdfService`): Generates printable 70mm × 38mm sticker labels and batch A4 sheets (3×8 grid = 24 labels/page) with QR code tokens.
- RFID / NFC Support: Native 13.56 MHz RFID/NFC tag UID association for sacred metal vessels and high-value furnishings.
- Mobile Field Audit Scanner (`_AuditScanDialog`): Continuous audit workflow using live camera scanner, NFC reader, or manual/USB barcode lookup with autocomplete suggestions.
- Archive Quarantine: Decommissioned assets are soft-archived while preserving historical audit inspection logs (`AssetAuditLogModel`).

8. SMART ARCHIVE ENVIRONMENTAL TELEMETRY (ESP32 IoT)
================================================================================
The Smart Archive Module provides real-time micro-climate monitoring for sacramental
record vaults, vessel storage, and reliquaries to prevent deterioration of historic ledgers
and sacred artifacts.

Conservation Standards & Thresholds:
- Temperature Range: Ideal 18.0 °C – 24.0 °C (Warning above 26.0 °C)
- Relative Humidity (RH) Range: Ideal 45% – 55% RH (Warning above 60% RH to prevent mold growth and paper degradation)

Hardware Nodes & Monitoring:
- Nodes (e.g. `ESP32-NODE-01` in Sacramental Archive Room) transmit real-time DHT22 sensor telemetry to Supabase.
- Visual warning indicators highlight environmental threshold breaches.
